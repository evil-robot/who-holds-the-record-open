# How stable the ranking is: uncertainty and sensitivity analysis

Generated by `analysis/robustness/robustness.py` from `robustness.json`; do not edit by hand. Run 2026-10-04, seed 20261002, 10,000 draws per experiment. Data: 198 country files in `data/` (asOf 2026-10-01, 2026-10-02), SHA-256 `7d9d4873c5fcc598...`; reconciled against `out/facts.json`: every published overall score and rank label is reproduced exactly before any draw is made.

Every interval here is a simulation under stated assumptions. The main model (`calibratedCombined`) is calibrated to measured disagreement: category-score error with SD 5.0 plus a shared country offset with SD 2.0 (the split suggested by the 2 Oct 2026 re-research of 43 countries), with the weights varied around the published ones. A cell-only calibration from the blind re-scoring's limits of agreement gives SD 6.24. The original uniform +/-5 model is kept as a lower bound on uncertainty.

## Findings

1. **Who leads.** Under the main model, Finland is first in 53.5% of draws. That is below the 95% rule (DECISION_RULES.md, rule 2), so no country may be called first alone. Countries whose 90% rank range includes 1: Finland, Denmark, Estonia, Hungary and Sweden (first in Finland 53.5%, Denmark 27.1%, Estonia 11.8%, Hungary 10.7%, Sweden 5.0% of draws).
2. **Rank ranges are wide.** The median 90% rank range is 43 places under the main model, 42 under the shared-error model without weight changes, 37 under the cell-only calibration and 17 under the uniform +/-5 lower bound. Neighbouring ranks should not be read as different.
3. **Band counts are soft.** Published: 9 Strong, 66 Mixed, 111 Weak, 12 Poor, 0 Leading. 90% ranges under the main model: Strong 7 to 14, Mixed 58 to 72, Weak 101 to 115, Poor 10 to 19, Leading 0 to 0. 100 of 198 countries hold their band in 95% or more of draws.
4. **The ends of the scale.** Share of main-model draws with any country at Leading (85+): 0.0%; with any country at Poor (24 or under): 100.0%. Highest score in any draw 83, lowest 8.
5. **What this does not test.** The choice of categories, the rubric anchors, the band edges, the keys model (a coded judgement), source selection and errors larger than the modelled ones. Correlated error from one model family doing research and checks is only partly represented by the shared country offset.

## Method

Source of method: OECD/European Commission Joint Research Centre (2008). Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators: Methodology and User Guide. Nardo M, Saisana M, Saltelli A, Tarantola S (JRC); Hoffmann A, Giovannini E (OECD). OECD Publishing, Paris. ISBN 978-92-64-04345-9. https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264043466-en. Opened 2026-10-02 (PDF SHA-256 `8b82c5b556312296...`).

- **rounding**: half up, as JavaScript Math.round in build.js
- **ranking**: competition ranking on the rounded overall, ties share the best rank, as build.js
- **bands**: applied to the rounded overall, edges as build.js BANDS
- **intervals**: 5th and 95th percentiles of the draws (inverted CDF, so every bound is an attained rank)
- **geometric**: weighted geometric mean of unscaled 0-100 scores (min score 20, no zeros)
- **leaveOneOut**: drop one category, rescale the other seven weights to sum to 100
- **dirichlet**: weights drawn from a Dirichlet with alpha equal to the published weight in points, published scores
- **band25**: each weight times an independent uniform factor in [0.75, 1.25], rescaled to sum to 100, published scores
- **band50**: each weight times an independent uniform factor in [0.5, 1.5], rescaled to sum to 100, published scores
- **noise5**: independent uniform on [-5, +5] per category score, clipped to [0, 100], published weights
- **noise5Shared**: pessimistic bound: one uniform [-5, +5] offset per country applied to all eight of its categories (a rater generous or harsh to a whole country)
- **combined**: Dirichlet weights and noise together in one draw (Handbook 7.1)
- **calibratedCell**: normal error per category score, SD 6.24 (blind re-scoring limits of agreement / 1.96 / 2 / sqrt 2), published weights
- **calibratedShared**: normal error per category score SD 5.0 plus one shared offset per country SD 2.0, published weights
- **calibratedCombined**: the calibratedShared error with Dirichlet weights in the same draw; the MAIN model for published rank ranges
